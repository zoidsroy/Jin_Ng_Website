    Created with game engines, *Boulder* is an audiovisual performance about labor, the artificial and the natural, the organic and the inorganic. Figures wander, push boulders, and hesitate — mirroring humanity in miniature.

    Combining game engines, computer music, and immersive sound technologies, *Boulder* seeks to deconstruct and reconstruct related contemporary issues, offering modern individuals a space to rethink their own existence, guiding the audience to drift between technological narratives and body perception.
