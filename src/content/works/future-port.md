  en: |
    The way we created this work by transplanting the city into the virtual world is similar to the way we used to create images by playing video games.

    We took Yanshui Hubi Temple as our source and reproduced the streets around it in the virtual world. We extended the idea of connecting the base of religious and ritual thinking about another world with the metaverse, and what kind of beliefs will exist in the future of humanity.

    The story follows a protagonist entering the virtual world through VR and searching for his mother in the Future Port, trying to realize that under metaverse life, the mutual influence of technology and affection will bring out another kind of emotion and longing. It is also an attempt to reflect on whether the changes in the living habits of contemporary life will turn a certain piece of emotion and culture into a puzzle that has been left behind.
  zh: |
    我們將城市移植到虛擬世界中作為本次作品的創作方式，就像是以前打電玩遊戲的方式來製作影像。

    我們這次由鹽水護庇宮取材，將護庇宮周圍的街道樣態再現於虛擬世界中，並由此延伸到藉由宗教、儀式這種觀想另一個世界的基底出發，與 VR 虛擬實境所帶來的未來元宇宙生活狀態產生連結：現在的信仰在未來的生活狀態中，會以什麼樣態存在？也就是透過意識、精神的觀看方式，在一座既不存在於過去亦不存在於未來、處於另個時空下的虛擬之城中遊蕩這種想像。

    故事主軸以主角透過 VR 進入虛擬世界，在 Future Port 中尋找自己的母親這樣的敘事展開，試圖體現未來元宇宙生活下，科技與親情相互影響所帶出的情感與思念的另種樣態，反思當代生活對於各種過往生活習慣的轉變，是否會讓某塊情感、文化成為被遺落的拼圖。
