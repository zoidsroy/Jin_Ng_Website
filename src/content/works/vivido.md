  en: |
    *VIVIDO* is XTRUX's ongoing new media performance and curation series, developed during the collective's residency at the Taipei Digital Art Center.

    The series has been staged in three editions: *VIVIDO* (FINAL, Taipei, 2023), *VIVIDO : Movement* (Taipei Digital Art Center, 2023), and *VIVIDO : re-Action* (Taipei Digital Art Center, 2024), which was also presented as an exhibition.
  zh: |
    《VIVIDO》是 XTRUX 於臺北數位藝術中心駐館期間持續發展的新媒體跨域表演與策展系列。

    系列共呈現三個版本：《VIVIDO》（FINAL，臺北，2023）、《VIVIDO : Movement》（臺北數位藝術中心，2023），以及《VIVIDO : re-Action》（臺北數位藝術中心，2024），後者亦以展覽形式呈現。
